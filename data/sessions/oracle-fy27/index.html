Abhishek Chetnani

Advisory | Managing Director | Mumbai

Abhishek Chetnani is a Managing Director in Oracle Finance Transformation, with deep expertise spanning solution architecture, AI enablement, and scaled Acceleration Center (AC) delivery.

He partners with clients to drive CFO-level outcomes through Oracle ERP, EPM, FDI, and reporting solutions, leading major transformation and expansion programs across Consumer Markets, Healthcare, Energy, and Financial Services. He has also scaled the Oracle EPM capability within the AC from 2 to 150+ professionals, building the delivery engine that now underpins the practice.

Abhishek has led & delivered enterprise-wide reporting solutions, including FDI, at large Healthcare clients such as LifePoint, Mayo Clinic, IU Health, and UW Health. He's known for stabilizing critical engagements and building repeatable, accelerator-led delivery models that others can scale.

Today, his focus is on advancing AI use cases in Finance & Analytics and integrating capabilities across platforms — elevating the AC's role from execution support to a strategic transformation partner.
